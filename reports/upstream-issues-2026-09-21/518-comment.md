We independently encountered the same final-token duplication in browser normal streaming at `a8cb07c` while integrating SmolLM2-360M. Our public [downstream patch](https://github.com/benjamin-small/stix-meta-explorer/blob/b86a73eddfae8a5603c49e5ade8af760bc84048c/patches/flare-smollm2.patch#L13-L43) changes both `begin_stream_impl` and `begin_stream_async_impl` to prefill the first N−1 tokens and process the final token once at N−1, including the one-token case. It does not fix or claim coverage of every core/batch entry point listed in this issue.

The patch is carried in STIX Meta Explorer, not a standalone Flare fork. With our accompanying loader/GPU repairs, eight short prompts matched llama.cpp text/token IDs in CPU/async, CPU/healed, GPU/async and GPU/healed modes (32 cases, eight unique prompts). These are combined-patch smoke tests; empty prompts, zero max_tokens, and all other acceptance cases here still deserve dedicated upstream coverage. [Public validation notes](https://github.com/benjamin-small/stix-meta-explorer/blob/b86a73eddfae8a5603c49e5ade8af760bc84048c/docs/stix-agent-validation.md).
